La Nuit is a 12-room boutique hospitality property in the Medellín highlands, developed by CHIHIZA Studio. The project targets premium international travelers seeking nature-immersive experiences with modern comfort. The investment requires USD 470,000 for a 25% equity stake, with a projected IRR of 19.4% and break-even by Year 4.

Management & Operations

How La Nuit will be operated

La Nuit will be operated by CHIHIZA under a long-term Hotel Management Agreement (HMA). CHIHIZA will be responsible for the complete day-to-day management of the property, including commercial strategy, guest experience, financial management, maintenance, marketing, and operational reporting.

During the initial operating phase, La Nuit will maintain a lean on-site team supported by CHIHIZA centralized management. This structure minimizes fixed costs while ensuring professional oversight in finance, marketing, asset management, investor reporting, and strategic decision-making. As occupancy grows, additional operational positions will be incorporated according to predefined performance milestones.

Methodology

Projections are built on a bottom-up model. Room revenue is calculated as: Rooms × Occupancy × ADR × 365. Operating expenses are modeled at 62% of revenue (industry benchmark for boutique hotels). Capital expenditures are amortized over 10 years. All figures are in USD. The model is available for review in the Supporting Documents section.

Investor Rights & Protections

A transparent governance framework for the La Nuit investment

The investor owns the asset. CHIHIZA operates the business.
This separation of ownership and operations is intended to protect the investor's capital while allowing a specialized hospitality operator to maximize long-term performance, guest satisfaction, and asset value.

A Transparent Governance Framework

The proposed investment structure is designed to align the interests of the investor and the operating company. While the investor owns and protects the real estate asset through PropCo S.A.S., CHIHIZA manages the day-to-day hotel operation through OpCo S.A.S. under a long-term Hotel Management Agreement (HMA). This separation provides legal clarity, operational efficiency, and long-term asset protection.

Investment Risks

We disclose risks transparently because we believe it builds trust

Risk Impact Probability Mitigation
Construction delays Medium — pushes break-even by 3–6 months Moderate (30%) Fixed-price construction contract with penalty clauses for delays. Contingency budget of 10%.
Cost overruns Medium — reduces Year 1–2 margins Moderate (25%) Detailed construction budget with line-item tracking. Monthly budget reviews with investor.
Lower-than-expected occupancy High — delays break-even and reduces IRR Low-Moderate (20%) Pre-opening marketing campaign. Partnership with booking platforms. Flexible pricing strategy.
Currency fluctuation (COP/USD) Low — all revenue is in USD; most costs are in COP Moderate (40%) Hedging strategy through forward contracts. Revenue is USD-denominated, providing natural hedge.
Regulatory changes Low — hospitality regulations in Colombia are stable Low (10%) Local legal counsel retained. All permits and licenses being secured before construction.
Climate events Low — property is in a low-risk zone for extreme weather Low (15%) Property insurance includes natural disaster coverage. Site selected for minimal flood and seismic risk.

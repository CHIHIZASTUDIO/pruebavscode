The Methodology

Every CHIHIZA project follows a structured process — not because we like bureaucracy, but because discipline produces better architecture.

Our methodology was developed from five academic theses, tested across multiple sites in Colombia, and refined through years of practice. It is not theoretical. It is the actual workflow we use for every project.

The process is transparent. You will always know where your project stands, what decisions have been made, and what comes next.

Step 01

Discovery

It begins with a conversation. Not a sales pitch — a genuine exploration of your vision, your timeline, your budget, and what you actually need.

We ask the hard questions early: Is this the right land? Is the timing right? Is this the right investment for you?

Sometimes the answer is no. And we will tell you that.

If the answer is yes, we move forward with a clear scope of work, transparent pricing, and a timeline you can hold us to.

Step 02

Territory Analysis

Before we design anything, we study the land.

Slope. Orientation. Wind patterns. Water flow. Soil composition. Existing vegetation. Solar exposure. Microclimate.

We use topographic surveys, environmental analysis, and direct site observation to understand what the land wants to be. This is where our research methodology becomes tangible — the same principles from our published theses, applied to your specific site.

The territory analysis produces a site report that becomes the foundation for every design decision that follows.

Step 03

Masterplan

With the territory analysis complete, we develop the masterplan — the strategic layout of buildings, circulation, green spaces, and infrastructure across the entire site.

This is where we determine building placement, density, orientation, and the relationship between built and natural environments. The masterplan ensures every individual structure serves the whole — and the whole serves the land.

For multi-building developments, this phase includes phasing strategy, infrastructure planning, and long-term land stewardship frameworks.

Step 04

Architecture

Design begins with the landscape, not despite it.

Every floor plan, every elevation, every material choice emerges from the site analysis. We design spaces that breathe — where the boundary between interior and exterior dissolves, where natural light and ventilation reduce the need for mechanical systems, where local materials connect the building to its place.

Our architectural process is iterative. You review. We refine. The design evolves until it feels inevitable — as if the building could not have been designed any other way for this specific piece of land.

Step 05

Engineering

Beautiful architecture means nothing if it does not perform.

Our engineering phase translates design intent into structural reality. Foundation systems, structural calculations, MEP coordination, waterproofing, thermal performance — every technical detail is resolved before construction begins.

This is where Julián David's expertise in project management and construction economics ensures that the design is not only architecturally excellent but structurally sound and financially viable.

Step 06

Construction

Construction is where vision meets reality. Our team manages every aspect of the build — material sourcing, contractor coordination, quality control, and schedule management.

We prioritize local materials and local labor. This is not just an ecological choice — it is an economic one. Sourcing within 50km reduces costs, supports local economies, and ensures materials are suited to the climate.

Throughout construction, you receive regular updates — progress photos, schedule reports, and transparent documentation of every decision made on site.

Step 07

Delivery

Delivery is not the end. It is the beginning of the relationship.

When the project is complete, you receive comprehensive documentation — as-built drawings, material specifications, maintenance guides, and warranty information.

But more importantly, you receive a home that works. A home that was designed for this land, built with materials from this region, and engineered to perform for decades.

Our relationship does not end at handover. We remain available for questions, maintenance guidance, and future phases of development.

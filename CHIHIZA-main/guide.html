The Guide

Everything you need before making the move

This is not a sales brochure. This is a comprehensive, honest, and practical guide to help you understand what it truly takes to buy property and build a life in Colombia.

Whether you are a foreign investor looking for opportunities, a retiree seeking a better quality of life, or a family considering relocation — this guide covers every angle.

We wrote this because we believe that informed decisions lead to better outcomes. When you understand the legal framework, the cultural nuances, the financial realities, and the practical steps — you move forward with confidence.

This guide is free because our goal is not to sell you something. Our goal is to help you make the right decision — whether that decision involves CHIHIZA or not.

The Beginning

Not every project begins with a spreadsheet.

Some begin with a mountain.

La Montaña is born in the highlands of La Vega, Cundinamarca — where the cloud forest meets the sky and the air carries the scent of wet earth and eucalyptus.

Here, the land does not ask to be tamed.

It asks to be listened to.

The slope.
The mist.
The orientation of the morning sun.
The ancient trees.
The sound of water in the ravine.

Every decision must be a response to the place.

This is not about building a hotel.

It is about creating a different way of inhabiting the mountain.

The Vision

Fifteen eco-lodges. One mountain. A single purpose.

Each lodge is conceived as a refuge — a private universe where the boundary between interior and landscape dissolves. 40 square meters of built space, a private terrace, and a view that belongs only to the guest. Simple, honest materials. Nothing superfluous.

A communal pool replaces private plunge pools. A modest Club House with a small restaurant and shared workspace. Basic viewpoints woven into the trails. Two kilometers of paths that become the circulatory system of the project.

The ambition is not luxury. It is integrity.

The mountain is not the backdrop.

The mountain is the project.

The Opportunity

La Montaña is not a single project.

It is the first project of a platform.

CHIHIZA is building the capability to identify, structure, develop, and operate eco-hospitality assets across Colombia. La Montaña is the proof of concept — a template that can be replicated in other landscapes, other climates, other territories.

The mountain is not the destination.

The mountain is the beginning.

What We Offer

To an investor, La Montaña represents:

Three exit paths — sell, operate, or partner. Each with a different risk profile and timeline.

Real assets in a stable country — land in Cundinamarca with clear title, legal framework, and growing tourism demand.

A differentiated product — not a generic hotel, but an ecological architecture project that competes on design and experience, not on price.

A team that has already built — Montvento exists. The methodology works. La Montaña is the next scale.

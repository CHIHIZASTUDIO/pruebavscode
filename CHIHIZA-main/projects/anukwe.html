Executive Summary

Opportunity Overview

ANUKWE is a private eco-hospitality development located in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, Colombia. The project combines conservation, sustainable tourism, and long-term value creation through a private equity investment structure.

Situated on 22 hectares of protected natural environment, ANUKWE offers a unique value proposition: direct equity participation in a luxury eco-hospitality asset with significant appreciation potential driven by Colombia's growing international tourism market.

The investment structure is designed for institutional and accredited investors seeking exposure to Latin America's sustainable tourism sector with clear exit pathways through asset appreciation or strategic sale.

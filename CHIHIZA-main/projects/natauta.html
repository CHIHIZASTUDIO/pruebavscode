Natautá

Private Nature Reserve

15 years of restoration. Now ready for Phase 2.

For 15 years, Natautá has been a living ecological restoration project. 22 hectares of Andean forest in La Vega, Cundinamarca — recovered, protected, and nurtured back to life. The guadua bamboo stands tall. The orchids bloom in the canopy. The birdsong returns at dawn.

The land is restored. The ecosystem is thriving. The papers are clean — title confirmed, POT/EOT permits approved for tourism and residential use.

Now comes Phase 2: transforming this restored forest into a world-class luxury eco-lodge destination.

The vision is15 luxury eco-lodge units distributed across the 22-hectare reserve — each one a CHIHIZA-designed structure in guadua bamboo and volcanic stone, integrated into the forest without disturbing it. Trails wind through the reserve. Experiences are curated: forest bathing, birdwatching, botanical walks, night hikes, artisan workshops. The central lodge houses a restaurant showcasing local produce.

Natautá is not a ground-up development. It is the rare case where the hardest part — restoring the land, securing the permits, establishing the ecosystem — is already done. What remains is the construction phase: building the lodges, the trails, and the experience layer that converts ecological value into premium hospitality revenue.

Investment Structure

Total project value: USD 2,140,000 (COP 9,000,000,000) — includes the 22-hectare restored reserve (Phase 1) plus full eco-lodge development (Phase 2).

Natautá operates on transparent investment structures designed for international investors:

  • Proindiviso (co-ownership): Direct undivided share (cuota parte) registered on the property title. Each investor holds a proportional interest in the 22-hectare estate.
  • Fiducia (trust): The property can be held in a Colombian fiducia trust, with the investor as beneficiary. Provides asset protection, simplifies transfer, and offers tax planning advantages.
  • Revenue-share: For investors who prefer not to hold title directly, profit-share arrangements tied to the lodge's operating performance are available.
  • Escrow protection: All funds held in Colombian fiducia account until milestones verified
  • Clean title: Certificado de Tradición y Libertad confirmed — no liens or disputes. Papers up to date.
  • Approved land use: POT/EOT permits for tourism and residential use in place
  • Phased payments: Deposit at contract, milestones during development, balance at delivery
  • Resale flexibility: Right of first refusal for existing investors; CHIHIZA facilitates secondary sales of cuota partes

CHIHIZA experience: Operating since 2020, with 3 completed projects in Colombia. GN Group specializes in premium projects ($500K–$800K USD range) across the country. Papers are up to date.

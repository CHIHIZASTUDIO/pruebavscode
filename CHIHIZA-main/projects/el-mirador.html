Overview

Valley views, mountain air, Colombian highlands

El Mirador sits in the highlands of Guatavita, Cundinamarca — a landscape of rolling green hills and panoramic valley views. At 2,600 meters above sea level, the climate is cool, the air is clear, and the connection to nature is immediate. This is Colombia's countryside at its finest, one hour from Bogotá.

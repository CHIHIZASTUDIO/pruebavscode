Executive Summary

The Complete Capital Architecture

When evaluating an institutional-grade land banking asset in Colombia, precision in capital structuring is paramount. Guadu comprises 42.7 total hectares with 50% permanently protected forest.

Phase II (Lots 77 through 126, representing 50 premium residential lots across ~65,000 m²) requires a foundational land acquisition value of 9,000,000,000 COP (~$2,142,857 USD). To unlock its maximum potential, capital must also account for horizontal infrastructure and vertical construction (eco-lodges / villas).

This memorandum outlines three distinct monetization scenarios for Business Angels and Family Offices, denominated entirely in USD (exchange rate benchmark: 4,200 COP/USD).

Scenario 1

Pure Land Banking & Serviced Lot Liquidation

Minimum capex strategy focused solely on land acquisition and horizontal urbanization.

Capital Required: $2,142,857 (Land) + $700,000 (Infrastructure & Working Capital) = $2,842,857 USD total.

Execution: The investor acquires the 9B COP land asset, implements basic gated access and road infrastructure across Phase II, and liquidates the 50 serviced lots to retail second-home buyers.

Revenue & Profitability: Selling 50 lots at an average retail price of $140,000 USD each yields $7,000,000 USD in gross revenue.

Scenario 2

Full Buildout & Eco-Resort Hotel Operations

Maximum value-creation strategy: Land acquisition + building 50 branded eco-lodges for nightly rental.

Capital Required: $5,000,000 USD total outlay (Land + Infrastructure + 50 Built Units).

Execution: The investor retains full ownership of the entire Phase II platform, operating it as an integrated ultra-luxury nature resort with central amenities, wellness spa, and gastronomy.

Revenue & Profitability: Operating 50 eco-lodges at an average daily rate (ADR) of $320 USD and 55% average annual occupancy generates robust recurring cash flows.

Scenario 3

Hybrid Model — Phased Development & Institutional Exit

The balanced institutional approach preferred by private equity and family offices.

Execution: The investor acquires the land ($2.14M USD), builds a seed cluster of 10 flagship eco-lodges and central club amenities to establish brand equity and cash flow, and retains the remaining 40 serviced lots for secondary market appreciation or wholesale bulk sale to a hospitality fund by Year 3.

Why it works: Mitigates execution risk, generates immediate rental yield from day one, and leverages land appreciation while holding a scarce asset in a protected ecological corridor.

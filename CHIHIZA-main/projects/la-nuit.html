Context

Colombia’s tourism opportunity

Colombia receives an average of 6 million non-resident visitors annually (24 million between August 2022 and today, 95% arriving through Bogotá). Tourism is concentrated in a few well-known spots — the Coffee Region, the Caribbean, the Llanos, and the Amazon — where natural traffic is high but competition is fierce.

Yet vast potential remains underdeveloped. El Gualivá, due to its proximity to the capital, is one of these frontiers. It offers everything from páramo lakes at 3,000 metres to tropical lowlands, passing through intermediate cloud-forest climates like that of La Vega.

Within Colombia’s rural tourism market, the premium niche — blue ocean — stands apart from the mainstream glamping boom (red ocean: low investment, increasingly commoditised). Premium projects combine the sensation of adventure and maximum escape with first-class comfort guarantees. They target high-purchasing-power locals (Bogotá) and international travellers seeking unique experiences.

6M annual visitors · 95% via Bogotá · 24M between Aug 2022 – today · Premium rural = blue ocean

Where we are today

La Nuit is a 5-star luxury eco-lodge in operation in La Vega, Cundinamarca. The property is titled, permitted, and built to the highest standard: luxury furnishings and linens, professional-grade kitchen, architectural finishes in guadua bamboo and volcanic stone. It is an exceptional asset — currently operated by its owners as a personal, lifestyle-oriented project rather than a fully commercialised hospitality business.

This explains why the numbers do not reflect the asset’s intrinsic value. The current operation carries deliberate restrictions — couples only, weekends only, no pets, no children — limiting occupancy to 13%. The property was never operated to maximise revenue. Yet it has built a track record of excellence: outstanding 5-star reviews on Google, TripAdvisor, and Booking, generating significant goodwill that a professional operator can capitalise on immediately.

There is no defect in the asset. It is an opportunity to unlock commercial potential through staged investments based on the three fundamental variables: Occupancy, Rate, and Units.

Location Intelligence

Why La Vega

Beyond the numbers, there is a place. And the place tells the story.

La Vega sits in Colombia’s temperate zone — a micro-climate with no seasons, no tourism seasonality, and 365 days of stable occupancy potential. It is a 90-minute drive from Bogotá through landscapes that rival the world’s finest mountain scenery. For international visitors accustomed to seasonal constraints, Colombia’s thermal floor climate is a revelation: consistent, predictable, always hospitable.

The northern slope of La Vega — where La Nuit is located — has been transitioning from agricultural use to tourism development since the POT (land-use plan) change a decade ago. The municipality has made tourism a strategic priority, leveraging its proximity to Bogotá, its lagunas, and its waterfalls. With the new national government reinforcing tertiary road projects and tourism infrastructure in the region, land appreciation potential is significant — and none of the returns above account for it.

The area is a mini coffee region, with native coffee minifundios offering rural cultural tourism experiences. It is also a birdwatching paradise, with La Vega’s local expert association actively promoting avitourism — a high-purchasing-power niche that aligns perfectly with La Nuit’s premium positioning. The global wellness tourism market is growing at double-digit rates annually, and Colombia’s premium eco-lodge segment remains under-supplied.

Premium niche is less competitive. The mainstream hospitality market is saturated. The ultra-premium wellness & eco-lodge segment is not. It allows for differentiated value creation, higher rates, and stronger guest loyalty.

Investment Structure

Transparent. Protected. Designed for international investors.

Legal clarity: The Certificado de Tradición y Libertad confirms the title is clean — free of liens, disputes, or encumbrances. Land use permits (POT/EOT) are officially approved for tourism and residential use. Papers are up to date.

Ownership structures — flexible for investor needs:

  • Proindiviso: Direct co-ownership share registered on the property title. Each investor holds a proportional undivided interest (cuota parte) in the estate.
  • Fiducia (trust): The property is held in a Colombian fiducia trust, with the investor as beneficiary. Provides asset protection, simplifies transfer, and offers tax planning advantages.
  • Revenue-share: For investors who prefer not to hold title directly, profit-share arrangements tied to the lodge's operating performance are available.

Escrow protection: All investor funds are held in a Colombian fiducia account. Funds are only released upon verified milestone completion.

Resale: Investors may transfer their cuota parte at any time. CHIHIZA facilitates secondary market transactions and maintains a right of first refusal.
